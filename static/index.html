    Child Nutrition Guidance

    Explore general food sources and nutritional information to support awareness of balanced child nutrition.

    Food recommendations are general educational information, not personalized medical advice. Consider the child's age, allergies, feeding stage and dietary needs. Consult a qualified healthcare professional if a nutritional deficiency is suspected.

    The screening model does not assess nutrient deficiencies.

    Iron-Rich Foods

    Iron helps the body make hemoglobin, which carries oxygen in the blood.

    Food examples: Lentils, chickpeas, beans, leafy green vegetables, and iron-fortified foods.

    Food variety: Include different suitable iron-rich foods as part of varied meals.

    Vitamin A Food Sources

    Vitamin A supports normal vision and the immune system.

    Food examples: Carrots, pumpkin, sweet potato, dark green leafy vegetables, and eggs.

    Food variety: Offer different colorful vegetables and other suitable foods across meals.

    Calcium-Rich Foods

    Calcium helps build and maintain bones and teeth and supports normal muscle function.

    Food examples: Milk, curd, cheese, and calcium-fortified foods.

    Food variety: Choose suitable options based on age, allergies, dietary restrictions, and family food practices.

    Protein-Rich Foods

    Protein helps the body build and repair tissues and supports growth.

    Food examples: Lentils, beans, peas, eggs, milk and curd, and fish.

    Food variety: Combine different suitable protein sources over time; prepare foods safely for the child's age.

    Vitamin C Food Sources

    Vitamin C supports healthy tissues and helps the body absorb iron from plant foods.

    Food examples: Guava, amla, oranges, and tomatoes.

    Food variety: Offer locally available fruits and vegetables in safe, age-appropriate forms.

    Folate-Rich Foods

    Folate helps cells grow and supports the formation of red blood cells.

    Food examples: Spinach, other leafy green vegetables, beans, peas, and lentils.

    Food variety: Include different suitable vegetables and legumes as part of varied meals.

    AI-generated results are intended for preliminary educational screening and should not replace professional medical assessment.
